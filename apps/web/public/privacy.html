Task Manager - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Task Manager is a personal productivity app built and used by a single person (its owner). It is not offered to the public. Only the Google account of the owner can sign in.

What the app accesses

After the owner signs in with Google, the app may access, using the owner's permission:

  • Basic profile information (name and email address), to confirm who is signing in.
  • Gmail: reading messages, to turn actionable emails into tasks, and creating email drafts. The app never sends email on its own.
  • Google Calendar: reading and writing events, to keep tasks and calendar events in sync.

How data is stored and used

  • Data is used only to provide the app's own features to its owner (tasks, calendar sync, daily summary, job-search tracking).
  • Tasks and settings are stored in a private database. Google access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete all stored data by deleting the app's database.
